LXC - School Management System SAAS
LXC is an advanced School Management System (SMS) that automates academic, administrative, and student engagement processes. Unlike traditional systems that focus only on attendance and grading, LXC integrates AI, gamification, real-time analytics, and blockchain security to create a modern educational ecosystem.
🛠 Version 1 (V1) - Core System Launch
The foundation of Academics Pro, V1 ensures that schools have all essential tools for smooth academic and administrative operations.
1️⃣ School Registration (Admin Level)
Multi-User Management: Admins can register and manage different user roles (students, teachers, parents, library, transport, hostel, finance/accounting staff).
School Dashboard Customization: Each school gets its own dashboard to track activities, events, and analytics.
Holiday Calendar Management: Admins can create, update, and notify users about school holidays and important dates.
Payment Gateway Integration: Secure fee payments using UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking.
Event Management System: Schools can create and manage events, send reminders, and track participation.
Attendance, Grades & Fee Analytics: Real-time insights into attendance records, student performance, and fee collections.
Automated Fee Reminders: SMS/email notifications for pending payments and deadlines.
2️⃣ Student Portal
Attendance Tracking: Students can view their daily attendance records.
Assignment Submission & Access: Digital assignments can be uploaded and submitted via the portal.
Class Schedules & Study Materials: View class timetables, and access subject-wise study resources.
Grade Reports & Teacher Feedback: Detailed performance analysis with teacher comments.
Online Fee Payment: Students can pay tuition fees directly through the portal.
Library Management: Request books, check availability, and track borrowing history.
Hostel & Transport Tracking: View hostel room allocation, meal plans, and bus routes with live tracking (if implemented).
Student-Teacher Chatroom: Secure real-time chat for resolving doubts.
Daily Quizzes & General Knowledge Updates: Engage students with interactive quizzes and current affairs updates.
3️⃣ Teacher Portal
Manage Classes & Schedules: Teachers can create and manage their weekly class schedules.
Grading & Feedback System: Assign grades with custom feedback for students.
Fee Summary View: Teachers can monitor class-specific fee status.
Attendance Marking: Quickly mark attendance for each class.
Study Material & Assignment Uploads: Upload PDFs, videos, and notes for students.
Extra Classes & Events Scheduling: Set remedial classes, workshops, or special sessions.
4️⃣ Parent Portal
Monitor Child’s Performance: Parents can track grades, attendance, and fee payments.
Transport Tracking: Real-time updates on the child’s school bus location.
Report Cards & School Notices: Easy access to performance reports and school announcements.
5️⃣ Library, Transport, Hostel & Accounts Portals
Library: Issue and return books with automatic due-date reminders.
Transport: Manage bus routes, schedules, and live tracking.
Hostel: Book rooms, track hostel fees, and view meal plans.
Accounts: Fee collection, payroll, and financial reports.
6️⃣ Additional Features
Video Conferencing: Integrated Zoom-like video calling.
